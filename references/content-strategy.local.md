## What the ranking rewards (from the code, not folklore)
- Score = weighted sum of predicted actions. Highest positives: copy-link share 20,
  reply 5, quote 5, DM share 5, follow 4. Like 0.5. Video views 0. Bookmarks unscored.
- Negatives dominate: not interested -47.5, mute -59, report -234. One ragebait
  post can cost more than ten good ones earn.
- Reply weight 5 -> 20 between mutual follows. Build real mutuals in the niche.
- Cold-start boost: authors <=50k followers, posts <200 impressions and <2h old.
  The first 2 hours decide a post.
- Links aren't penalised, but link posts earn ~58% less engagement. Put links in a reply.
- Extra posts from the same author decay in a feed: few strong posts > many weak.

## Formats that win in this niche (measured)
63/89 top posts contain a specific number. 2/89 use hashtags. Hooks are a claim
(47) or a number (38); questions open only 4. Most use 2-4 short blank-line beats.
1. Identity + ask: "I'm 21, YC backed solo founder in London. Looking to connect
   with technical builders" (112k views, 98 chars). Short, personal, specific.
2. Number journey: "I sat at $100 MRR for 6 months. today: $15k MRR. nothing viral
   happened. I just did 4 things" / "what actually got me my first 100 users".
3. Sharp observation, list rhythm: "X is full of founders. Instagram is full of
   creators. LinkedIn is full of experts. Where are the customers?" (88k, 110 chars).
4. Operator behind-the-scenes in creator marketing: "We contacted ~220,000
   influencers to build Cal AI's creator program." Real ops detail, real number.
5. "I built X" with visible output: "i built a skill to un-slop AI-looking apps" (321k).

## NEVER
- "Bookmark this 🔖 🧵👇", "repost for others", follow/comment-for-DM bait.
- Fabricated quotes ("Anthropic engineer: 99% of people...") or invented numbers.
- Politics, ragebait, dunking on people: mutes and "not interested" are the heaviest weights.
- Hashtags. Tagging strangers. Links in the main post.
- Generic AI news repeats; react only with a first-hand take.
